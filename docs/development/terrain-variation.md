# Horizontal terrain variation

The marked Skyweave preset composes horizontal outlines, fragment fringes, and fractured exteriors over Sky Archipelago 1.4's native land and ocean bodies. [Terrain](terrain.md) owns geological mapping, interiors, hydrology, structures, and saved-world boundaries. [Configuration](../configuration.md#island-outlines-and-fractured-terrain) owns field defaults and application timing; [verification](verification.md) separates actual native observations from visual tuning and balance.

## Geometry and ownership

`ShapeProfile` is an immutable startup description. `IslandGeometry` selects seeded aspect and retains native orientation. For equivalent radius r and aspect a, axes r√a and r/√a approximately preserve area; integer rounding and native shape masks still affect realized land. Ordinary cluster spacing, center jitter, activation, elevation, and the native vertical taper retain their owners.

`IslandStyles` resolves ecology and outline variation using independent salts and a bounded field-local cache. `OceanShapeMixin` applies broad horizontal warp before the native shape mask. Crescent openings and crater intent remain part of the retained body.

`FragmentFringes` adds density lobes outside that body rather than subtracting notches from its useful land. Mixed profiles choose coherent smooth and granular perimeter sectors; Disintegrating profiles increase coverage and maximum reach while retaining quiet sectors. Broad periodic noise varies local reach below the conservative maximum, avoiding a continuous uniform fringe. The lobe kernel provides seeded positions, varied elliptical cell radii, local warp, independent fragment height/depth and outward decay. Cell-center distance is estimated from the native local boundary normal. Deterministic thinning and shrinking radii make outer remnants smaller and more widely separated on average; no concentric tiers or per-column grid rescaling are introduced. A small separation admits detached shoulders. `FringeColumn` carries fragment metadata separately from native horizontal coverage; `ShapeDensityMixin` builds its own vertical interval, within the envelope, and retains the native core's broad taper. A bounded underside accent adds small points rather than replacing the body sampler.

`FragmentedExterior` remains the separate biome-aware broken-land profile: bounded jittered cells, joins, missing remnants and shared remnant heights. Fragments retain their parent descriptor, geological frame and structure allowance. They do not create new resource or landmark budgets. Added fringe columns retain BASELINE interiors; ordinary fractured bodies retain their configured NOISE/HOLLOW behavior and shell checks. Special Deep Dark bodies select HOLLOW where the remnant can retain the configured shell; thin remnants stay solid.

Placement and native plan bounds include rotated aspect changes, broad displacement, fringe reach and companions before clearance. The field's same accepted membership reaches fill, height/column queries, structures, metadata previews and consumers. A rejected ocean candidate cannot reserve phantom clearance. Query order, eviction and worker scheduling must not change the result. Conservative larger bounds can reject a neighboring candidate; unchanged spacing settings alone do not prove unchanged realized cluster counts.

Potentially fragmented columns require exact snapshot sampling: four empty corners cannot prove that the intervening cell contains no remnant. Proven-empty candidate-index regions retain the empty fast path. Compare exterior, pre-water interior, completed columns and saved blocks separately. Only the complete canonical `outline`, `fringe`, `fractures` and `deepDark` schema is accepted. Unsupported, partial or incorrectly typed profiles fail without inserting guessed values. Opening an editor can normalize its draft without rewriting a startup file.

## Biomes and water

Fracture geometry is independent of NOISE/HOLLOW interiors and surface ecology. Mushroom Fields retain native mycelium and mushrooms; Badlands retain terracotta and dry vegetation. Configured biome IDs admit a fractured alternative without changing every island of that biome. Foreign biome namespaces keep their own surface and decoration ownership.

The rare Deep Dark profile requires native underground Deep Dark climate eligibility and the cave-biome adaptation. It retains fractured geometry and native biome identity. `DeepDarkMaterials` resolves the actual solid contributor at physical Y: both native filling and base-column queries select deepslate throughout its stone body, including inherited fringe material. Existing ore draws keep their budgets and use corresponding deepslate variants; filler, fluids, air and seals retain their own rules. Ordinary underground Deep Dark does not trigger this whole-body replacement.

The natural surface hook paints a coherent sculk cap with exposed deepslate interruptions before structure/foundation provenance is captured. It is separate from decoration. Thick special remnants use the existing bounded HOLLOW cavern, pillars, shell and openings; cavity air is not refilled by the material rule. The surface mask uses the existing seeded value noise and a configurable bias/scale, not independent per-block draws or an exact coverage quota. Natural cavern walls and undersides remain rock. Later native ore/features can still change their own eligible material.

Each chunk has independent bounded draws for at most one vein, sensor, catalyst and summon-capable shrieker, with 16 candidate attempts per selected kind. Placement requires actual air above eligible exposed rock for veins or sculk for devices, native survival and no intersecting referenced structure piece. Unknown or over-budget structure references suppress this small decoration pass without loading chunks. A full natural sculk cap does not multiply devices. The former native sculk-patch pass is removed; `deepDark` owns the cap and decoration settings.

Natural tree/vegetation and spreading-sculk configured features are excluded at their special-habitat origin. Native sculk-patch and sculk-vein growth also carry a bounded call scope: generation-region writes entering the special habitat from a neighboring origin are refused. Gameplay catalysts and authored features remain outside that scope. Simple plant features inspect the provider's already-selected state, without another random draw. Native jigsaw piece placement carries a nested authored scope, retaining template-authored features even when ground support is disabled. Passive natural/chunk-generation spawn predicates are excluded within the habitat; command, breeding, existing animals and player farming remain native. These are bounded native feature/spawn boundaries, not a universal classifier for arbitrary modded renderers or generators. Dependency updates must recheck their call sites, feature identities and authored scope.

No Ancient City is required or forced. Ordinary distribution, burial, cavernization and support rules still decide whether a city fits, including on a special island. Terrain admission does not depend on city availability or the global structure setting. Nuit and sky transitions remain separate Skyweave-mod work.

Ocean admission retains the native eligibility tag. A failed probability roll leaves void, matching the native all-ocean-archetypes-disabled outcome. Marked-preset ocean weights and Lagoon shoreline treatments come from the owned ocean profile; ordinary upstream presets keep their own settings. Broken Reef is not reused as a water enum for fractured land.

Separate solid spires may accompany ocean anchors. They remain outside native wet/rim support, enter cluster bounds and clearance, and use parent geology. They do not enable ordinary land satellites or alter the anchor's underside. Native shore projection, water material, seals, fluid ticks, and local spawning checks retain their separate responsibilities. A planned shoreline mode does not prove that a waterfall has flowed in saved terrain.

Dry ocean fringes inherit material roles from their actual winning surface contributor: sandy Lagoon shores or Broken Reef stone/calcite/sandstone. `SurfaceProvenance` uses native exposed palettes; native required-soil fallback would turn an all-stone reef selection into dirt. `IslandSurfaceScope` caches one column per painting call and leaves lower overlays, land palettes and water membership unchanged. No synthetic basin, fluid or river outlet is created for material selection. Recheck the native dominant-contributor, snapshot, surface-write and palette contracts on Sky Archipelago updates.

## Island metadata locator

Operators can inspect accepted plans without loading or generating chunks:

```text
/skyweavecompat islands search 24000 archetype=lagoon shoreline=perimeter
/skyweavecompat islands status
/skyweavecompat islands page 2
/skyweavecompat islands cancel
```

The search captures its origin and dimension. Filters compose as unique `key=value` arguments; suggestions expose native archetypes, `exterior=native|fractured|deep_dark`, `interior=baseline|noise|hollow`, `shoreline=none|native|coastal|perimeter`, biome IDs, and families. The default family is `anchor_plateau`; `family=any` includes companions. Non-native Lagoon shoreline filters cannot be combined with another archetype. `fringe=none|mixed|disintegrating` separately filters dry outer-fragment styles on land or ocean hosts; it can combine with `archetype=lagoon shoreline=coastal`. The retained `coastal` command value identifies the open-cove shore treatment.

Searches continue automatically in worker slices of at most 256 cells or two seconds, yielding through the server tick scheduler between slices. Search bounds allow at most 131072 blocks and 65536 cells; overly dense configurations require a smaller radius. The cursor retains at most 64 nearest planned centers, ten per page. The full finite search must finish to establish those nearest results. Status and pages remain available while running; these pages are provisional. No match so far differs from completed absence. Use `status` to observe progress without enqueueing more work.

Only one search runs per server. Another requester cannot replace active work. Starting a new search after completion replaces the retained result set. Cancellation, player disconnect or dimension change, dimension unload, and server shutdown cancel pending work. Native field planning runs on a bounded metadata worker; immutable snapshots published by server-thread callbacks own status and paging. Progress uses the player action bar at most every two seconds or console messages at most every ten seconds; completion prints one result page in chat. The worker receives immutable settings and the already-configured field, never chunk tickets or a mutable world-generation request. Review the exact native cache and biome-sampler concurrency contracts on dependency updates.

Result rows show representative coordinates, horizontal distance/direction, and the main island profile. Hover reveals biome, family, modifiers and identity. Clickable coordinates only suggest a teleport command for review; previous/next/cancel actions execute the corresponding locator command. A center may be void or unsafe for landing. Planned terrain is not a claim that chunks are saved, unmodified, or that fluid has flowed. Foreign or disabled generators are unsupported. The command neither teleports nor starts pregen.

## Structure discovery

Pending metadata batches and native requests retain their shared server-wide allowances until completion, including after cancellation. Repeatedly replacing a job cannot accumulate additional outstanding scans or native requests.

Minecraft's random-spread lookup enumerates potential chunks before presence resolution; it does not first call the owned `isPlacementChunk` override. `ArchipelagoStructurePlacement.applyAdditionalChunkRestrictions` shares the distribution's selected-site rejection with native lookup after loaded/saved-start checks. Unselected void candidates can therefore be rejected without requesting STRUCTURE_STARTS. Before fitting a full cluster plan, the same sampled area, biome eligibility, allowance, and ranked attempt budget used by fitting reject impossible family/site pairs. This avoids fitting unrelated surface families for a city search with no eligible city site. Full fitting, family priority, conflicts, and successful starts retain their existing behavior.

An unbound distribution must not publish a cached negative based on provisional fitting. Runtime context binding remains authoritative. Preserve canonical relocated positions, saved starts, references, skip-known behavior, native bypasses, disabled adapters, and unknown structures. Finding a positive city and rejecting void requests do not establish all rare-search latency or structure frequency; retain bounded cold/warm and missing-result checks.

Ownership rejection uses pre-water exterior metadata. Native generation and legacy lookup share the same authoritative fitted plan and ranked-attempt bounds. This avoids a separate unverified fitting cache; the bounded structure command does not use native synchronous presence lookup as its precheck. Completed terrain inside a fit can still invoke cold watersheds. Qualify exact village variants with Dynamic Waters, absent-city and saved-start controls; passing metadata rejection checks alone does not establish safe synchronous search latency.

This is a discovery repair, not a rarity or watchdog-policy change. Two original watchdog reports showed synchronous STRUCTURE_STARTS waits; only one also had active DH workers. Those observations did not establish a rarity regression, deadlock, or DH as the sole cause. Use isolated fixtures rather than repeating watchdog crashes on a playing server.

### Bounded command

`StructureLocator` resolves family/ID/tag selectors once, then uses the actual placement owner. Managed families enumerate accepted cluster owners and generation's finite ranked attempt prefixes; foreign random-spread placements retain native-grid enumeration. Repeated placement references are normalized without merging distinct targets, salts or locate offsets. Exact IDs reuse family candidates; native fitting determines the selected variant. Mixed tags combine supported routes and explicitly qualify unsupported placements. No placement grid means saved-start discovery only.

Loaded, updating and unloading holders are inspected on the server thread. A separate bounded storage path inventories existing region headers and pending writes on the storage owner's serial queue, then uses native streaming NBT scans to identify possible selected starts. It never opens a second region-file reader or creates missing regions to test absence. Current new-generation rejection cannot hide a saved start, including one outside today's lattice or without an active placement. Older/unknown NBT formats go through native loading instead of becoming cached negatives. Native valid starts remain authoritative; metadata is only a filter.

`StructureSearchStorageMixin` uses vanilla `IOWorker.submitTask`. C2ME replaces that worker with `C2MEStorageVanillaInterface` and nulls the inherited queue/storage fields. The optional `c2me.structure_search` adapter delegates to `C2MEStorageThread.executor`, incorporating both its cache and write backlog. Its exact supported module is `c2me_rewrites_chunkio` 0.4.0-alpha.0.124+1.21.1. Absent C2ME, foreign classes are not resolved. If an installed replacement lacks the selected bridge, saved coverage is qualified as incomplete; it must never be reported absent or complete.

Per-cluster fitting publishes immutable ordered prefixes. Earlier families retain their reservations and exact random/attempt rules; later families are deferred. Unknown retained overlap bounds may require the complete plan. An unbound provisional fit is never negative evidence for native lookup. Query caches belong to the generator state and retain existing bounded eviction; saved positives remain outside speculative generation policy.

Native verification requests only `STRUCTURE_STARTS`, using the pinned thread-aware `getChunkFuture` worker branch and minimum-status tickets. Ticket mutation and result inspection remain server-owned. Metadata/native allowances survive job cancellation until their callbacks finish, so replacement jobs cannot accumulate unbounded work. Late callbacks cannot publish into a replacement job.

The first verified match is published immediately; retained pages use actual locate X/Z and start-bounds Y when native Y is zero. Results are distance ordered among verified matches, without claiming globally nearest coverage. `status` separates cluster visits, saved-region/chunk checks, pending requests and aggregate planning/storage/native-request times. Native-request time includes queueing and fitting; those totals overlap and must not be added as wall time. A time or candidate/placement limit is partial. Only exhaustion of the supported new and saved domains is complete. Discovery observes a live world, not an atomic historical snapshot of concurrent generation.

Recheck Minecraft/NeoForge placement and serialization fields, region-cache/header ownership, native IO scheduling, C2ME backend/cache/backlog contracts, ticket levels, offsets, tags, cancellation and callback lifetimes on updates. Use positive exact-ID/tag/family queries, saved-only reopen controls, direct/exhaustive candidate equality, real eager/lazy/concurrent/evicted layout equality, absent targets and budget exhaustion. `tools/probe.py --structure-search --search-planning --reopen` adds bounded planning equivalence and forces new-start rejection on reopen. Synchronous native callers do not inherit responsiveness guarantees from this command.

## Deferred Gargantuan islands

Gargantuan is a distinct special type with independent 4000–6000-block diameter and provisional 1/150 selection per activated cluster opportunity. It is not a larger LARGE band and must not inherit ordinary size settings. This idea is documented, not scheduled for implementation here.

It needs its own placement/reservation layer, conservative bounds, finite spatial lookup, cache budgets, biome policy and structure-density review. A 1024-chunk-radius disk contains about 146 current grid cells, or 124 activated candidates before clearance, so 1/150 implies roughly 0.8 candidates in that area before overlap rejection. Neither that rate nor a nominal 3×3/4×4 footprint guarantees one island or a valid reservation. Reserve actual bounded influence, including distortion and companions; keep the existing ordinary grid untouched.

The 4–6 km diameter exceeds current Compat radius validators and native configured radius limits. Enlarging all lookup ranges would impose a cost on ordinary chunks. Broken Reef planning also contains all-cell searches that require measurement before scaling. A 5 km disk has about 69 times a 600 m disk's footprint; resource abundance and useful travel need review independently of its rarity.
